Zentra systems switcher

A single floating control that lets anyone jump between the five live Zentra systems without typing a URL.

What this preview proves. The switcher below is the exact file that will be installed on the five live systems. This page only stands in for them. Nothing has been published to the live systems yet.

Please test in this order

  1. Click the Zentra systems pill, bottom-left. Five systems should appear; this page is not one of them.
  2. Check the wording of each system's one-line description.
  3. Press Esc, click outside the panel, and click the ×. All three must close it.
  4. Toggle Light / Dark (button bottom-right) and look at the pill and panel again.
  5. Narrow the window to phone width (~415px). It must stay clear of the page content.
  6. Tab through the panel with the keyboard — focus ring must be visible on every row.
