2 Terms of service: what the portals actually say

PropertyGuru / iProperty

The iProperty acceptable-use policy bans “any automation software, hacks, mods or any other unauthorized third-party software designed to access, crawl or collect” from the site. PropertyGuru's policy on porting listings states their systems detect and block third-party access and that bots cannot be granted access on request. This is the single hardest constraint in the design.

Mudah.my / Carousell

Mudah's terms prohibit scraping, framing and spidering without written consent, and assign copyright of listing content to Mudah. The PRO Niaga dashboard does allow drafting and then publishing, which is exactly the shape an assistive tool can fit.

Meta & WhatsApp

Page publishing through the Graph API remains available. Group publishing does not: publish_to_groups was removed from all Graph versions on 22 April 2024. The Marketplace partner API covers European countries only, and Meta policy bars business sellers from Marketplace. WhatsApp Business accounts cannot post into groups at all.

3 The pragmatic reading

The sanctioned surface is wider than it looks. Feed aggregation (Dot Property, Trovit, Mitula, Nestoria), our own sites, the Facebook Page, Telegram channels and Google Business Profile together cover the reach that pages of manual posting used to. The two portals that matter most for buyers — PropertyGuru and iProperty — are reachable through one sanctioned move: AgentNet cross-listing, which duplicates a PropertyGuru record onto iProperty without any third-party tooling. That is a portal feature to switch on, not software to build.
