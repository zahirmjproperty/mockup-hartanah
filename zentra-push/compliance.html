Compliance guard

The rules that stop Zentra Push from doing something clever, and what happens in the software when a channel is refused.

This is the page that makes Zentra Push different from the tools it replaces. Competing products authenticate to portals with the agent's credentials and post on their behalf, which is precisely what the largest portals forbid. Here the refusal lives in code: a channel marked as prohibited simply has no automatic route, and the console shows it greyed out rather than quietly attempting it.

3 Advertising and identity obligations

Registered identity on every ad

The firm registration and the negotiator's own registration number belong on advertising material. Zentra Push injects them from the agent profile at write time so a listing can never go out un-attributed.

No contact details in body copy

Major portals strip or penalise phone numbers and e-mail addresses inside the listing description. The renderer removes them before submission and relies on the platform's own contact fields instead.

Personal data

Listing records carry owner information. Storing portal passwords server-side would widen that exposure for no benefit, which is why assist mode runs inside the agent's own browser session instead.
