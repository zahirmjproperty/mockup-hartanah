Feed, API, assist and portal-switch adapters, capability declared per channel.

3 What the build deliberately does not do

No portal credential vault

“Just log in as the agent and post” is the obvious shortcut and the one that turns ten minutes of work into an account suspension. Refused.

No scraping of portal listings

Importing a competitor's or a portal's listing into our own database is expressly prohibited by the terms we would be importing from.

No automating the portals that matter most

PropertyGuru and iProperty are reached through cross-listing and assist sessions, not through a bot. That is a permanent design decision, not a temporary one.
