E · Compliance & Audit

DL/APDL, eSPA-HIMS, e-invoice, PDPA, bumi and access control

Compliance is enforced as a gate, not a reminder: if a rule fails, the affected action is blocked and the failure is logged with an owner. Nothing here is advisory-only.

A project page may only be published when, at minimum: the developer licence and advertising permit numbers are recorded, the permit is valid today, the authorised agent (ejen diberi kuasa) matches our appointment, and the price list version is dated. Otherwise the page stays noindex with a visible compliance notice — never a soft “coming soon” teaser that still markets the project.
