How this replaces the current CRM board

Today the pipeline lives in a Google Sheet (Pipeline tab, cards PL-####). In ZENTRA Contact the same stages become rows in PostgreSQL, so a card can link to the contact record, documents, consent and audit trail — instead of being a row that only references a name. The Sheet can stay as a read-only mirror during the twin period.
