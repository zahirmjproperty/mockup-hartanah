Why this matters

The current setup already sends an idle-card alert to Telegram once a day. ZENTRA Contact makes the reminder a first-class record: it belongs to a contact, has an owner and a due date, and appears on that contact's timeline when it is closed. That is what turns "we alerted you" into "it was done".
