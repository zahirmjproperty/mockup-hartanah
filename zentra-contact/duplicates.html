How matching works in the live system

Phone (normalised to +60), email (lower-cased) and IC are compared — never name alone, because two different people can share a name and the same person is often stored with a different spelling. A merge keeps the chosen primary record, moves the children (phones, emails, interactions, documents) across, and archives the loser instead of deleting it, so the merge can be undone. The current contact database already de-duplicates on phone and email — that rule is carried over rather than reinvented.
