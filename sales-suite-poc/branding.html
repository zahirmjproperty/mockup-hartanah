3. Where the brand appears — and where the legal name must

e-Invoice rule (LHDN): the invoice must carry the registered legal name and TIN of the issuing entity. A trade name cannot substitute for it. Current instruction: issue under ZMJ Solutions and display “Zentra Property Group” as the trade brand; the suite must support switching the issuing entity — including its TIN — when a new group company is registered.

Screening verdict: the name is usable — the exact-match .com is free, which is the strongest asset — but three collisions must be managed: (1) ZENTARA GROUP Sdn Bhd (one-letter difference) can block or draw an objection at SSM; (2) Zentra Group plc is a listed UK residential property company, so never shorten the brand to “Zentra Group”; (3) “Zentra” is already a live Malaysian property project (Seremban), so always market as the full three words — Zentra Property Group — with the full name in titles, listings, OG tags and domain.
Mitigations to apply: (a) run the SSM name search and a MyIPO Class 35/36 check before any printing or incorporation; (b) register zentrapropertygroup.com and .my, plus .net defensively; (c) lock the wordmark/typography so it cannot be confused with Zentara/Zentra Group plc; (d) keep the brand as a trade name under the registered entity until the new group company is incorporated — invoices always carry the registered name + TIN.
