D · Billing & Commission

Statutory milestone billing plus event-based commission release

Schedule G (landed) and Schedule H (strata) agreements under the Housing Development (Control & Licensing) Act 1966 dictate when money may be collected. Below is the statutory payment schedule for a Schedule G agreement — the billing engine follows it exactly and refuses early billing.

Late-payment interest: typical agreements allow 10% p.a. on overdue instalments after 21 working days' notice. The billing engine therefore calculates the notice date, the grace period and any interest automatically — and never sends a demand without the architect certificate attached.
