Proof of Concept

New-launch sales system for Mr Tanah · client-facing brand: Zentra Property Group · English (US)

What this is: a proposed sales system for new property launches, operated by Mr Tanah (internal admin) and presented to clients as Zentra Property Group — “System provided by Zentra Property Group”. The suite name is Zentra Launch. Language: English (US) client-facing, with a Malay (ms-MY) layer for internal MT operations.
Why not just buy a developer system? Mr Tanah is the appointed sales & marketing agency, not the developer. Developer platforms (MHub, PropertyX/IFCA) own unit inventory and HIMS/eSPA generation. The agency layer — leads, agents, temporary unit locks, booking packs, loan follow-up, document compliance and commission — is where Mr Tanah needs its own system, and where it must plug into the developer platform rather than replace it.

Core design principles (10)

  1. No client money at Mr Tanah. Reservations are RM0 (EOI/OTP). The 10% deposit reaches the developer's HDA only after a signed SPA.
  2. One unit, one lock. Every lock has an owner, a timer and an audit record — no double selling.
  3. Enter data once. The same record feeds HIMS/eSPA, invoicing and commissions.
  4. Compliance gates, not reminders. Missing DL/APDL, expired permits or unconsented data block publication.
  5. Buyer-side transparency. Buyers choose their own lawyer (RFQ tender) and financier; Mr Tanah takes no kickbacks and adds no fees.
  6. Role-based portals. Token links per buyer, seller, agent, lawyer, banker — the same pattern already used by the Mr Tanah portal.
  7. Immutable audit trail. Who locked, booked, changed price or released a unit — recorded forever.
  8. Build on existing assets. Notion databases, Google Workspace, Apps Script, WhatsApp, Billplz/DuitNow QR, AI assistant.
  9. Modular 5 phases. Each phase delivers standalone value.
  10. KPI driven. Lock→booking time, loan approval rate, time-to-SPA, take-up per phase.
