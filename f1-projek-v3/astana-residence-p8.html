No money is collected at this stage. Under Regulation 11(2) of the Housing Development (Control & Licensing) Regulations 1989, no payment may be taken before the Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA) is signed. Your interest is logged with a queue reference only; the 10% deposit is paid after the SPA is signed, into the developer's Housing Development Account.

What happens next

  1. Submit this form (RM0) — you get a queue reference by WhatsApp/email.
  2. Talk to an agent — project briefing, showroom appointment, unit availability check.
  3. Confirm the unit — when you are ready, a temporary lock is placed on that unit. Still no money.
  4. Sign the SPA — digital SPA through KPKT HIMS with eKYC and e-stamping; the 10% deposit is then paid to the developer's Housing Development Account.
  5. Financing — apply to your chosen bank; the system tracks submission, consent and approval.
  6. Construction & billing — milestone billing per the Third Schedule, visible in your portal.
  7. Vacant possession — handover, defect list, defects-liability period.
