3 Rules the demo follows

Lead source is captured, not guessed

Each campaign writes its own source tag onto the lead, so the cost per lead below is arithmetic, not an opinion.

Bookings, not clicks

The column that matters is bookings. A campaign with many replies and no booking is a cost, not a success.

Consent before send

WhatsApp and e-mail sends require a recorded opt-in on the contact — the same rule the booking E-Form consent step enforces.
