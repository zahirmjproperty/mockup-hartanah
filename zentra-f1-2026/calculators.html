Stamp duty (MOT) and loan agreement

MOT bands used: 1% on the first RM100,000 · 2% on the next RM400,000 · 3% on the next RM500,000 · 4% above RM1,000,000. Loan agreement: 0.5% of the loan. First-time buyer exemptions are not applied here.

RPGT on a later sale

Individual citizen rates: 30% in the first three years, 20% in year four, 15% in year five, nil from year six. Companies pay a flat 10%. Costs of sale (agent fee, legal fees) are excluded here.
