Executive Summary

Quarterly performance at a glance, the main findings, and the decisions required from the reader.

The third quarter of 2026 recorded the strongest transaction performance of the year, with a materially shorter closing cycle than the previous quarter.

Closed value rose to RM 4.82 million across 27 units, against RM 4.09 million and 24 units in the second quarter. The conversion rate improved from 9.6% to 11.4% after the qualification filter was tightened in July 2026, while the median closing period fell from 51 days to 42 days. This chapter summarises three things: headline numbers, two observations that need attention, and the decisions recommended at the end of the document.

Key Findings

  1. Higher-quality enquiries. The conversion rate improved from 9.6% to 11.4% after the qualification filter was tightened in July 2026.Qualification filter: financing eligibility is pre-checked before viewing appointments are scheduled.
  2. Shorter closing cycle. The median fell to 42 days from 51 days; the largest single factor was earlier collection of financing documents.
  3. One unresolved risk. Dependence on two financiers covers 61% of cases; this exposure is discussed in Chapter 5.
Decision Required

Approve the Chapter 6 recommendations: (a) enforce the four-viewings-per-week cap per negotiator, and (b) open two additional panel financiers before year-end. Both are low-cost and act on the two findings above.

Method note

Segments are assigned on the date the sale and purchase agreement is signed. Transactions that change segment mid-process are counted once, under the segment recorded at signing.

Process and Workflow

The workflow actually followed, and the quality checkpoints that keep figures reconcilable.

  1. Enquiry intake. Every enquiry is registered with its source channel and a response-time stamp.
  2. Qualification. Financing eligibility is pre-checked before any viewing is scheduled.
  3. Viewing and offer. Offers are logged with the date, unit and agreed price.
  4. Documentation. Financing documents are collected within seven days of the offer.
  5. Closing. The signed agreement is reconciled against the register before the month is closed.

Quality Checkpoints

  • Every closed figure traces back to a signed agreement reference.
  • Month-end reconciliation is signed by two people.
  • Response-time stamps are checked weekly, not monthly.
  • Financing approvals are tracked per financier, not in aggregate.

Observations and Risks

What the figures do not show, and the exposure that needs a decision.

Concentration Risk — High

Two financiers account for 61% of all closed cases. A single policy change at either institution would affect most of the pipeline. Recommended action: open two additional panel financiers before year-end.

Data Quality — Medium

Nine enquiries in the quarter were registered without a source channel, which weakens any channel-level comparison. This is a process gap, not a system gap.

Confirmed Causes

  1. The response-time stamp is optional in the current intake form — hence the missing channels.
  2. Financing documents are collected by three different people with no shared checklist.
  3. Panel financiers were last reviewed in 2022.

Numbers are only comparable when the process behind them is identical every month. Our advantage this quarter was discipline, not demand.
