Documents & e-sign

Agents create the letter, clients sign it — no printing, no meeting, no courier. The system generates the PDF, collects signatures with an OTP, and keeps a soft copy both sides can download or print later.

1 How a letter travels

1 · Agent createsPick template, fill the property and parties. Fields auto-fill from the deal record. < 2 minutes.
2 · System generatesNumbered PDF (ZTH-LOAR-000123), timestamp, SHA-256 hash, QR verification block.
3 · Parties signWhatsApp / email link in signing order. OTP confirms identity. Each signature is stamped with time, IP and device.
4 · Completed & filedCompletion certificate issued, bundle saved for 7 years, soft copy sent to every party.

The 30-day clock starts on the date the last party signs — the system reads that timestamp from the signing log, so nobody has to remember it. Late stamping costs RM50/10% then RM100/20%.

6 What the system refuses to e-sign

  • Blocked by law: power of attorney · wills and codicils · trusts · negotiable instruments
  • Blocked by land registration: Memorandum of Transfer (Form 14A) · charges · registered leases — these must be signed in wet ink and registered; the system hands them to the panel lawyer
  • Allowed: letter of appointment · letter of offer · letter of confirmation · tenancy agreement (unregistered) · booking forms
