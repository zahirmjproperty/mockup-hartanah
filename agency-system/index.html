Zentra Hub — Overview

Agent management for Zentra Property Group, built around the IQI payout structure: a declared negotiator commission capped at 40% of the professional fee, plus a Marketing Bonus paid out of the agency share for level and leader incentives.

1 How a payout splits

Sample: rental at Seri Harmoni, Kajang · professional fee RM1,018.52 · negotiator level 85% · leader 15% — the exact shape of the vouchers issued on 30 Jun 2026.

Commission 40%
Bonus 45%
Leader 15%
Declared commission — RM407.41 Marketing Bonus (agent) — RM458.33 Marketing Bonus (leader) — RM152.79
LineAmount% of fee
Negotiator's commission declaredRM407.4140.0%
Marketing Bonus — agent levelRM458.3345.0%
Total to negotiatorRM865.7485.0%
Marketing Bonus — leader overrideRM152.7915.0%

Two separate documents are produced: the commission voucher (never above 40%) and the Marketing Bonus voucher. Both post to the agency's marketing account code.

Why two documents?

  • ✓
    Only 40% is called commission. The declared line stays inside the ceiling recognised in practice for negotiators.
  • ✓
    Everything above it is agency-funded. Level bonus and leader override come out of the agency share, labelled Marketing Bonus.
  • ✓
    Transaction-based. Every ringgit traces to a real deal — fee, invoice, voucher, unit reference.
  • ✓
    Audit-ready. The system prints "declared commission vs total paid" every month, so nothing is discovered late.
Design note: this mirrors a market practice (a gap in how the 40% ceiling is worded). The engine keeps rates as configuration, so the agency can move to a strict ≤40% model without rebuilding the system.
